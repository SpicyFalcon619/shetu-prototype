The pipeline

AI

1 · Listens

Her chat or voice, in Bangla, English or Banglish, becomes text.

Whisper large-v3 (speech) · typed chat in this demo

AI

2 · Builds the Role Map

A language model turns her words into a fixed structure: duty, when, where, clients, how critical. Each item keeps the words it came from.

Qwen3-4B, local, JSON mode

AI

3 · Cross-checks

Compares the Role Map with her job description and asks about anything missing.

Qwen3-4B + a rule that removes false alarms

RULES

4 · Finds clashes, checks fixes

Plain code: duties due during leave, time and travel clashes, policy, each colleague's spare hours. The AI drafts fixes; rules drop the bad ones.

Python rules engine

PEOPLE

5 · Decide

Her manager confirms each fix. HR backs anything left open. She sees every decision.

What it runs on

Models

Qwen3-4B open language model (6-bit, llama.cpp) and Whisper large-v3 for Bangla speech. No model is trained on employee data.

Runs offline

On a laptop GPU (RTX 4050, 6 GB): about 22 s per case. In a company it can run on its own server, so data never leaves.

Honest limits

Tested on one demo case so far. Accuracy will be measured in pilots: issues found that nobody listed, and fixes managers accept.
